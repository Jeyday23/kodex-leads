1. Welcome & Overview

What is Kodex Leads?

Kodex Leads is an outbound compliance prospecting dashboard built for Kodex partners. It automatically finds, scores, and enriches companies that need EU compliance help — so you can focus on outreach and closing deals.

The Compliance Opportunity

Two major regulatory forces are creating massive demand for compliance services:

EU AI Act

Regulation 2024/1689. Full enforcement began August 2, 2025. Every company deploying AI in the EU needs risk assessments, technical documentation, and human oversight procedures. Fines up to €35M or 7% of global turnover.

GDPR

Regulation 2016/679. Ongoing enforcement with increasing activity. Standard tier fines up to €10M or 2% of turnover. Serious tier up to €20M or 4% of turnover. Over €4 billion in fines issued since 2018.

How You Earn

15% Commission on Every Conversion

When a prospect you source signs up for Kodex Compliance, you earn 15% commission. Conversions are tracked automatically via Stripe and paid Net 30. You can monitor everything in the Conversions tab of your dashboard.

3. Understanding Lead Scoring

Every lead in the system receives a score based on signals that indicate compliance need. Higher scores mean stronger prospects. Leads scoring 40 or above are classified as Qualified and appear in the Prospects tab.

Scoring Breakdown

Signal Points Why It Matters
Uses AI in production +25 EU AI Act applies directly. Highest-value signal.
Team size 11–50 +20 Large enough for compliance obligations, small enough to need external help.
Team size 51–200 +15 Scale-ups with growing compliance complexity.
Recently funded (Seed–Series B) +15 Investors require compliance. Budget available.
Completed a Kodex assessment +10 Already aware of compliance gaps. High intent.
From job scraper or AI scraper +10 Actively hiring compliance roles or building AI products.
Business email domain +5 Real company, not a personal enquiry.
Assessment + AI + zero compliance +15 bonus Uses AI, took assessment, has zero measures. Maximum urgency.

How to Read Scores

Score 60+: Hot lead. Matches multiple high-value signals. Prioritise immediately.

Score 40–59: Qualified. Worth outreach. Look for the specific signals to personalise your message.

Score below 40: Not yet qualified. May become qualified as more signals are detected.

4. Daily Workflow

Follow this 8-step process to efficiently work through your leads each day.

1
Check the Prospects Tab Start each session by opening the Prospects tab. New leads appear automatically as scrapers run. Leads are ranked by score — highest first.
2
Review Lead Details For each prospect, check the company name, score, signals (AI usage, team size, funding stage), and any available contact information. Expand the card to see decision-maker contacts.
3
Claim the Lead Click “Claim” on leads you want to work. Claimed leads move to your Pipeline and are no longer visible to other partners. Only claim leads you intend to contact within 48 hours.
4
Research the Company Before reaching out, spend 5 minutes researching. Check their website, LinkedIn, and any recent news. Identify their specific compliance gaps. Use the Prospecting Sources page for research links.
5
Personalised Outreach Use the enriched contact data (names, titles, emails) to reach out. Reference their specific compliance gaps from the lead signals. Never send generic messages.
6
Share Assessment Tools Send prospects to the free assessment tools to generate urgency:
  • /assess/eu-ai-act — EU AI Act Readiness Assessment (shows their risk classification)
  • /assess/gdpr — GDPR Fine Risk Calculator (shows their fine exposure)
These tools create a concrete, personal urgency that generic sales pitches cannot.
7
Update Your Pipeline As you progress with each lead, update their stage in the Pipeline tab: Not Contacted → Emailed → Replied → Meeting Booked → Converted. Accurate pipeline data helps you forecast and prioritise.
8
Close and Earn When a prospect signs up for Kodex Compliance, the conversion is tracked automatically via Stripe. Your 15% commission appears in the Conversions tab. Payment is Net 30.

5. Using Sales Resources

The Resources tab contains five tools designed to help you sell more effectively. Here is when and how to use each one.

EU AI Act Battle Card

When: Before any call about AI compliance. During objection handling.

What it covers: August 2 enforcement deadline, risk classifications (High/Limited/Minimal), compliance requirements, penalty framework (up to €35M), and quick objection responses for “We don’t use AI”, “We’re too small”, “We already have GDPR”, and “The deadline is far away”.

GDPR Objection Handling Guide

When: When prospects push back on GDPR compliance need.

What it covers: Article 83 penalty framework (two tiers), 6 real enforcement cases you can cite (Meta €1.2B, WhatsApp €225M, H&M €35.3M, British Airways €22M, Marriott €20.4M, Clearview AI €20M), and 7 common objections with fact-based responses.

ROI Calculator

When: During or after a call, to put concrete numbers in front of the prospect.

How to use: Select the prospect’s team size, required frameworks, current approach, and data sensitivity. The calculator shows manual compliance cost vs. Kodex cost, fine exposure, annual savings, ROI multiplier, and break-even time. Use the “How to present these numbers” section for ready-made talking points.

Partner Sales Playbook

When: As a reference for your overall sales approach.

What it covers: The Kodex value proposition, ideal customer profile, discovery questions mapped to compliance gaps, the complete dashboard workflow, commission structure, and partner guidelines.

Prospecting Sources

When: When you want to find leads beyond what the scrapers provide.

What it covers: Links to automated sources (Arbeitnow, EU-Startups, Product Hunt, GitHub), manual prospecting channels (LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Crunchbase, GDPR Enforcement Tracker, Indeed/StepStone), search terms you can copy, and signal stacking criteria for manual qualification.

6. Outreach Best Practices

The Golden Rule

Lead with risk, not features. “Your fine exposure is €15M” is 10x more effective than “We have great compliance software.”

Email Outreach

  1. Personalise every message. Reference the company’s specific compliance signals: “I noticed you’re hiring a Data Protection Officer — that suggests you’re building out compliance capability.”
  2. Use the assessment tools as conversation starters. “I put together a quick EU AI Act risk assessment for companies like yours — takes 2 minutes: [link to /assess/eu-ai-act]”
  3. Follow up within 48 hours. First follow-up gets 3x the response rate of no follow-up.
  4. Keep it short. 3–5 sentences maximum for cold outreach. Link to an assessment tool for depth.
  5. Use real enforcement data. “Meta was fined €1.2B for data transfer violations last year. The same rules apply to companies of every size.”

Call/Meeting Tips

  1. Open with a question, not a pitch. “What AI or ML systems do you use in production?” — this maps directly to EU AI Act risk classification.
  2. Use the ROI Calculator live. Share your screen, input their details, and show them the numbers in real time.
  3. Address objections with data. Open the Objection Handling guide and reference specific enforcement cases and Article numbers.
  4. Close with a concrete next step. “Let me send you the assessment link. Once you complete it, I’ll put together a compliance roadmap specific to your company.”

What NOT to Do

  • Do not send bulk, impersonal messages. Every outreach should reference the company specifically.
  • Do not make claims beyond Kodex’s documented capabilities.
  • Do not contact prospects who have explicitly opted out.
  • Do not use lead data for any purpose other than direct compliance outreach.

7. Pipeline Management

The Pipeline tab shows your claimed leads in a Kanban board with five stages. Move leads through stages as your outreach progresses.

Pipeline Stages

Stage Definition Action Required
Not Contacted Lead claimed but no outreach sent yet. Send first outreach within 48 hours of claiming.
Emailed First outreach sent. Waiting for response. Follow up after 48 hours if no reply. Send a maximum of 3 follow-ups.
Replied Prospect has responded to your outreach. Engage in conversation. Share assessment tools. Schedule a call.
Meeting Booked A call or meeting has been scheduled. Prepare with Battle Card and ROI Calculator. Run the assessment beforehand if possible.
Converted Prospect signed up for Kodex Compliance. Conversion tracked automatically. Commission appears in Conversions tab.

Pipeline Best Practices

  • Update stages daily. An accurate pipeline lets you prioritise and forecast.
  • Don’t hoard leads. If you haven’t contacted a lead within one week, consider releasing it so another partner can work it.
  • Watch the “days in stage” indicator. Leads stuck in one stage for more than 7 days need attention or should be moved on.
  • Focus on “Replied” and “Meeting Booked” stages. These are closest to conversion and deserve the most attention.

8. Commissions & Payouts

15%

Default commission rate on every conversion

Net 30

Commission paid 30 days after confirmed conversion

How It Works

  1. You claim a lead and work it through your pipeline.
  2. When the prospect signs up for Kodex Compliance, Stripe automatically records the conversion and attributes it to your partner account.
  3. The conversion appears in your Conversions tab with the plan, MRR, and your commission amount.
  4. Commission is paid out 30 days after the confirmed conversion date.
  5. Payout status is visible in the Conversions table: Paid or Pending.

Conversions Tab Overview

The Conversions page shows four key metrics at the top:

  • Total Earned: Lifetime commission earned across all conversions.
  • Pending Payout: Commission confirmed but not yet paid (within Net 30 window).
  • Conversions: Total number of successful conversions.
  • Conversion Rate: Percentage of your claimed leads that converted.

9. Partner Guidelines

As a Kodex partner, you are representing the Kodex brand. These guidelines ensure professional, compliant, and effective partnerships.

1. Accurate Representation

Represent Kodex Compliance services accurately. Do not make claims beyond documented capabilities. If a prospect asks about a feature you are unsure of, say so and direct them to info@kodex-compliance.com.

2. Data Usage

Lead data provided through the dashboard is for direct, compliance-related outreach only. Do not resell, share, or use lead data for unrelated purposes.

3. GDPR-Compliant Outreach

You must comply with GDPR when contacting leads. You must have a lawful basis for outreach (legitimate interest for B2B compliance-related contact). Respect opt-out requests immediately.

4. No Bulk Extraction

Do not use automated tools for bulk extraction of lead data from the dashboard. The system is designed for manual, targeted prospecting.

5. Direct Contact Only

Contact prospects directly. Do not delegate outreach to unlicensed third parties or subcontractors without written approval from Kodex.
